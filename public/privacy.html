Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Real-Time Privacy Guard Architecture

🛡️ 1. Core Privacy Philosophy

SChat is engineered from the ground up on a fundamental commitment: your communications belong exclusively to you. We believe privacy is an intrinsic human right, not a setting to be toggled.

🔒 2. End-to-End & Ephemeral Messaging

Direct messages across SChat can be configured with ephemeral self-destruct timers. When a message expires, it is permanently wiped from active socket buffers and database tables with zero recovery capabilities.

🚫 3. Zero Third-Party Tracking & Advertising

Zero Ad Trackers SChat does not integrate Google Analytics advertising beacons, Facebook Pixel, tracking scripts, or ad networks. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your user activity, metadata, or contact graph to advertisers or data brokers.

🍪 4. Cookie & Local Storage Usage

SChat uses local browser storage exclusively for core operational functions:

  • Authentication Tokens (JWT): Stored securely to preserve your active session across reconnects.
  • UI Preferences: Preserving theme preferences (Dark vs. Light mode) and audio notification toggles.
  • Cache & Service Worker: Enabling Progressive Web App (PWA) offline capabilities.

📡 5. Media & File Uploads

Media uploaded to SChat (voice recordings, images) is scrubbed with automated expiration policies. Expired files are systematically unlinked and deleted in automated purge cycles.

⚖️ 6. Contact & Data Access Inquiries

For inquiries regarding privacy, data removal, or session purges, you can initiate a permanent session revocation directly via your in-app Settings > Devices panel, or reach out to the project administrator at support@schat.dev.
